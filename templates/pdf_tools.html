How this tool treats your document

  • Nothing is uploaded. The file is read with the browser's File API and written back out as a Blob. There is no upload endpoint, no storage and no logging of file contents.
  • Pages are copied, never redrawn. Content streams come across byte for byte. Nothing is rasterised and no page is rebuilt from scratch.
  • Annotations, form fields and layers stay live objects. Nothing is flattened, and the form-field appearance regeneration that would silently restyle a filled form is switched off.
  • Anything that cannot be preserved is listed. Every operation reports what was carried across, what had to be restructured and what could not come along — rather than dropping it silently.
  • Encrypted PDFs are refused, not unlocked. This tool does not remove PDF passwords or permissions.
